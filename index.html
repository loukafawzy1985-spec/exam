Section B: Multiple Choice DOK 15 marks

Choose the correct word to complete each sentence.

  1. The dog ________ loudly at the postman.
    1. bark
    2. barks
    3. barking

    ❌ We add -s to the verb for he, she, and it in the present tense.

  2. Yesterday, we ________ to the park to play.
    1. go
    2. goes
    3. went

    ❌ The action happened yesterday, so we use the past tense of 'go', which is 'went'.

  3. She is eating ________ apple for a snack.
    1. a
    2. an
    3. the

    ✅ Well done!

  4. Look at ________ birds flying high in the sky!
    1. this
    2. that
    3. those
  5. My older sister is ________ than me.
    1. tall
    2. taller
    3. tallest
